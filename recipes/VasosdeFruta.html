Vasos de Fruta

Vasos de fruta, or fruit cups, are a refreshing and healthy snack that can be enjoyed any time of the day. They are typically made with a variety of fresh fruits, often layered in a clear cup to showcase their vibrant colors. You can customize them with your favorite fruits, such as berries, melons, grapes, and citrus. For added flavor, you can drizzle a little honey or sprinkle some mint leaves on top. These fruit cups are perfect for parties, picnics, or as a quick grab-and-go snack.

Ingredients:

  • 2 bite-sized chunks watermelon, or more to taste
  • 2 bite-sized chunks cantaloupe, or more to taste
  • 4 green grapes
  • 4 red grapes
  • 2 strawberries
  • 4 blackberries
  • 2 chunks pineapple
  • 2 sticks jicama, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon chamoy sauce, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Tajin seasoning, or more to taste
