Cucumber Salad

This cucumber salad is a refreshing and tangy side dish that pairs well with a variety of meals. The combination of thinly sliced cucumbers, onions, and a sweet and tangy dressing makes it a perfect addition to any summer gathering or barbecue. The dried dill adds a burst of flavor that complements the crispness of the cucumbers.

Ingredients:

  • Cucumbers: Start with four thinly sliced cucumbers.
  • Onion: A thinly sliced white onion lends flavor and texture.
  • Vinegar: A cup of white vinegar gives the cucumber salad a bright flavor.
  • Sugar: White sugar lends subtle sweetness.
  • Water: You’ll need ½ cup of water.
  • Dill: Dried dill takes the flavor up a notch.
